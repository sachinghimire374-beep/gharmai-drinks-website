# 📖 Staff Guide — Managing Gharmai Drinks (No Coding Needed)

Everything on the live website can be changed from the **Admin Dashboard**.
You never need to touch code or ask a developer for day-to-day updates.

## Logging in
1. Go to **yourdomain.com/admin** (e.g. `gharmaidrinks.com/admin`).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You'll see a sidebar — the menu shows only what your role is allowed to manage.

| Your role | What you can manage |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Super Admin** | Everything |
| **Content Editor** | Products, Ads, Blog posts |
| **Order Manager** | Orders, Customers |

---

## ➕ How to add a product
1. Click **Products** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Product** (top right).
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (e.g. "Johnnie Walker Black Label")
   - **Price** in rupees (e.g. `5500`)
   - **Compare-at** (optional) — the old/"was" price to show a discount
   - **Category** — pick from the dropdown
   - **Description**
   - **Badge** — Premium / Popular / Limited / Value / New (or None)
   - Tick **Featured** to highlight it, **Sponsored** to mark it in the grid
4. Click **Save**. It appears on the website immediately.

## 🖼 How to upload a photo
- Inside any product, ad, or blog post there's an **Upload image(s)** button.
- Click it and choose a photo from your computer (you can select several at once).
- Or paste an image **URL** into the box and press Enter.
- The first product image is used as the main photo. Hover an image and click **Remove** to delete it.

## 📣 How to create an ad / banner / promotion
1. Click **Ads & Banners** → **+ Create Ad**.
2. Choose the **Placement** — where it shows on the site:
   - **HERO** — big rotating banner at the top
   - **TOP_BAR** — thin strip across the very top (great for flash sales)
   - **MID_PAGE** — wide banner between sections
   - **POPUP** — appears once after a visitor confirms their age
   - **FLASH_SALE** — countdown deal
   - **SPONSORED_CARD / SEASONAL / EXIT_INTENT** — extra placements
3. Add a **Headline**, **Subtext**, **Button Label**, **Link** (e.g. `#menu`) and a **creative image**.
4. Set **Start** and **End** date/time — the ad turns on and off **automatically**.
5. Set **Priority** (higher number shows first when several ads share a spot).
6. Use **Frequency** for pop-ups (`once_per_session` or `once_per_day`).
7. Watch the **Live preview** at the bottom, then **Save**.
- Each ad shows its **views, clicks and CTR** on the card so you can see what works.
- Click **Disable** to hide an ad without deleting it.

## 🎟 How to create a coupon
1. Click **Promotions** → **+ New Coupon**.
2. Enter a **CODE** (e.g. `WELCOME10`), choose **Percent** or **Fixed** discount and a **value**.
3. Optionally set a **minimum order**, **usage limit**, and **start/end** dates.
4. Save. Customers type the code at checkout and the discount applies automatically.

## ✍️ How to publish a blog post
1. Click **Blog Posts** → **+ New Post**.
2. Add a **title**, **excerpt**, **cover image** and the **body** (you can paste formatted text/HTML).
3. Set status:
   - **Draft** — not visible
   - **Scheduled** — set a future publish date/time; it goes live by itself
   - **Published** — live now
4. Fill the **SEO meta title/description** to help Google.
5. Save. Live posts appear at **yourdomain.com/blog**.

## 📦 How to handle orders
1. Click **Orders**. New orders arrive here automatically (and on your WhatsApp).
2. Use the dropdown on each order to move it through:
   **Received → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered**.
3. Click **View** to see the full address, map link, items and the exact WhatsApp message.
4. Filter by status using the dropdown at the top.

## 👑 How to manage VIP members
1. Click **Customers**. Everyone who has ordered appears here.
2. Click **Manage** to set their **VIP tier** (Silver / Gold / Platinum / Black Card) or adjust **reward points**.

---

### Tips
- Changes are **live instantly** — refresh the website to see them.
- Use **Featured** and **Sponsored** sparingly so they stand out.
- Schedule ads in advance (e.g. a Friday-night flash sale) and they'll run on their own.
- If something looks wrong, **Disable** it rather than deleting — you can re-enable later.
